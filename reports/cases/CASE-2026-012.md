## Summary

Classic DeFi oracle manipulation attack. Attacker used flash loans to manipulate prices in permissionless pools, then exploited Makina's oracle reliance on these pools for lending/borrowing. MEV bots front-ran the attacker and captured most of the stolen funds, leaving the attacker with a fraction. Demonstrates ongoing vulnerability of permissionless oracle designs to flash loan manipulation.
