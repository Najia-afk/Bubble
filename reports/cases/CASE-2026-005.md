## Summary

GANA Payment protocol exploited for approximately $3.1M+ on BSC. The attacker deposited 1,140 BNB ($1.04M) to Tornado Cash on BSC and bridged funds to Ethereum. On Ethereum, 346.8 ETH ($1.05M) was deposited to Tornado Cash. An additional 346 ETH ($1.046M) remains dormant.

## Key Findings
