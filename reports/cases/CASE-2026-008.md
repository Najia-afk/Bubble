## Analysis Notes

### What Worked
- Investigation skill completed all 6 steps without errors
- 323K+ transfers fetched successfully from blockchain
- Risk scoring correctly identified CRITICAL risk level
- Exchange endpoint detection identified 42 cash-out wallets

### What Needs Attention
- **ML model not used**: All classifications came from heuristic rules, not the ML model
- **86% classified as "exchange"**: This seems high — may need to differentiate between legitimate exchange addresses (like Uniswap router) and actual cash-out addresses
- **Zero mixer/bridge detection**: Either the attacker didn't use mixers/bridges, or our detection needs expansion

### Recommendations
1. Cross-reference the 42 "exchange" wallets against known DEX/CEX databases
2. Differentiate between DEX router contracts and actual exchange deposit addresses
3. Investigate the 6 "normal" wallets — could be intermediary or attacker-controlled
4. Check if the $54.7M address is a known Uniswap or DEX contract
5. Re-run with higher depth (depth=3) to find potential second-layer laundering
