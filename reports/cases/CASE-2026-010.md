## Summary

Attacker deployed a helper contract on SagaEVM that crafted fake IBC messages to the bridge precompile. The precompile trusted all incoming messages without verifying source-chain collateral, enabling infinite minting of $D (Saga Dollar). Minted tokens were redeemed against Colt and Mustang protocols for real yield-bearing collateral (yETH, yUSD, tBTC), then bridged to Ethereum via LayerZero. Funds converted to 2,000+ ETH via 1inch/KyberSwap/CowSwap. $6.2M through Tornado Cash, $847K parked in Uniswap v4 LP positions under a fresh wallet. Root cause traced to Ethermint codebase — affects multiple Cosmos EVM chains.
