## Summary

First major hack of 2026. Attacker exploited an unchecked integer overflow in TrueBit's unverified Purchase contract (deployed 2021, Solidity v0.5.3). The `getPurchasePrice()` function lacked SafeMath on one addition. Attacker minted 240M+ TRU for near-zero cost, burned for real ETH at 12.5% buyback rate, repeated 5 times atomically. All 8,535 ETH subsequently laundered through Tornado Cash. Same attacker previously drained Sparkle protocol for 5 ETH — a serial "relic hunter."
