## Summary

Largest social engineering attack of early 2026. Attacker compromised executive devices at Step Finance (likely phishing), transferred staking authorization to a fresh wallet, then unstaked 261,854 SOL from treasury and fee wallets. STEP token crashed 93.3% ($0.023 → $0.001578). $4.7M recovered via Token22 protections on Remora Markets assets. Majority of stolen SOL remains parked in attacker wallets. No EVM footprint — documented for cross-reference and pattern analysis only.
