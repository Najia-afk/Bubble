## Summary

$1.4M drained from TMXTribe due to a logic bug. Suspicious circumstances: team wallets deployed contracts during the exploit, never paused operations, and continued deploying contracts days later with complete radio silence. rekt.news flags this as a potential choreographed exit rather than an external attack. The team's operational behavior during and after the incident raises serious red flags.
