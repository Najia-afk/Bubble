## Summary

Victim had suspicious outflows of approximately $1.1M from five wallets on EVM chains. Attacker swapped all assets for ETH immediately and deposited 330 ETH to Tornado Cash. An additional 346 ETH ($1.046M) sits dormant.

## Analyst Assessment

This case shows a classic private key compromise pattern:
1. Multiple wallets from same seed drained simultaneously
2. Immediate token → ETH swaps (reduces traceability)
3. Partial Tornado Cash deposit (obfuscation)
4. Remaining funds dormant (waiting for heat to cool)
